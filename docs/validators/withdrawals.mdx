Withdrawals send ETH from your validator to its **withdrawal address**. The regular ones are automatic: you don't send a transaction, and they cost no gas. What gets paid, and when, depends on the validator's **withdrawal credentials**.

## Check your credentials

Ask your beacon node:

```bash
curl -s http://127.0.0.1:3500/eth/v1/beacon/states/head/validators/<VALIDATOR_INDEX_OR_PUBKEY> | jq '.data.validator.withdrawal_credentials'
```

The start tells you the type, and the last 40 hex characters are the withdrawal address:

| Credentials | Paid automatically | On request from the withdrawal address |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `0x00…` (legacy) | Nothing, until you [add an address](/validators/add-withdrawal-address/) | Nothing |
| `0x01…` (regular) | Everything above 32 ETH while active; the full balance after exit | Full exit |
| `0x02…` (compounding) | Everything above 2048 ETH while active; the full balance after exit | Full exit, partial withdrawal, consolidation |

Once set, the withdrawal address can't be changed.

## Automatic withdrawals

The network goes through all validators in turn and pays out what each one has to withdraw. On mainnet a full round takes several days, so expect a payment every few days to a week or more. You don't need to do anything.

- **Partial withdrawal:** an active validator's balance above its maximum (32 ETH for `0x01`, 2048 ETH for `0x02`) is paid out.
- **Full withdrawal:** after you exit, the whole balance is paid out.

Withdrawals aren't transactions. On a block explorer, look for them under "withdrawals" for your validator or address.

## Add a withdrawal address to a `0x00` validator

A `0x00` validator earns rewards, but nothing can be withdrawn until you give it a withdrawal address. See [Add a withdrawal address to a legacy BLS (`0x00`) validator](/validators/add-withdrawal-address/).

## Compounding (`0x02`) and consolidation

A compounding validator keeps its rewards staked, up to 2048 ETH, so they earn rewards too. It also lets you run one validator instead of several.

- **New validators:** create keys with `--compounding`. See [Create validator keys](/validators/create-keys/).
- **Upgrade a `0x01` validator** to `0x02` in place ([how](/validators/upgrade-to-compounding/)). This is one-way: afterwards, rewards above 32 ETH are no longer paid out automatically; you request a partial withdrawal when you want them.
- **Consolidate** validator A into validator B: A's balance moves to B, and A exits. B must have `0x02` credentials, and both must share the same withdrawal address.
- **Partial withdrawal** from a `0x02` validator: withdraw a chosen amount, as long as 32 ETH stays.

All four requests are sent **from your withdrawal address**, not from Prysm. The simplest way is **Validator actions** on the Staking Launchpad (`https://launchpad.ethereum.org/en/validator-actions`, or the same page on the Hoodi launchpad): connect the wallet that controls the withdrawal address, pick the validator and the action. Each request costs a small fee. When many requests are waiting, processing can take days.

## Check that it works

- **Credentials:** run the credentials check after each change.
- **Pending requests:** your beacon node shows queued partial withdrawals and consolidations:

  ```bash
  curl -s http://127.0.0.1:3500/eth/v1/beacon/states/head/pending_partial_withdrawals | jq
  curl -s http://127.0.0.1:3500/eth/v1/beacon/states/head/pending_consolidations | jq
  ```

- **Payments:** block explorers such as [beaconcha.in](https://beaconcha.in) list withdrawals per validator and per address.

## Troubleshooting

**No withdrawals arrive.** Check the credentials: nothing is withdrawn from a `0x00` validator. A new `0x01` validator has little above 32 ETH to pay out. A `0x02` validator pays nothing automatically until it exceeds 2048 ETH.
