Your validator exists once its ETH is deposited into the **deposit contract** together with your deposit data. Use the official **Staking Launchpad**: it checks your `deposit_data-*.json` file and sends the transaction from your wallet.

## Before you begin

- Have your `deposit_data-*.json` file. See [Create validator keys](/validators/create-keys/).
- Have your node ready: execution client and beacon node synced, keys imported, fee recipient set. A validator that activates while your node is down loses balance. See [Import keys](/validators/import-keys/).
- Have a wallet (browser extension or hardware wallet) with the deposit amount plus gas. On Hoodi, get test ETH from a faucet.

:::danger[Only use the official launchpad]
Scam sites copy the launchpad and send your deposit to their own contract. Type the address yourself or use a bookmark; never follow links from ads, search results, chats or emails.

| Network | Launchpad | Deposit contract |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Mainnet | <a href={mainnet.launchpad}>{mainnet.launchpad}</a> | <code>{mainnet.depositContract}</code> |
| Hoodi | <a href={hoodi.launchpad}>{hoodi.launchpad}</a> | <code>{hoodi.depositContract}</code> |

Before you sign, check that the transaction goes to exactly this contract address. You can compare it with [ethereum.org](https://ethereum.org/en/staking/deposit-contract/).
:::

Never send ETH to the deposit contract with a plain transfer or from an exchange. A deposit needs the signed data from your file.

## Deposit through the launchpad

<Steps>

1. **Open the launchpad** for your network (table above) and read through its checklist.

2. **Choose your clients.** Select Prysm and your execution client. This only tailors the launchpad's instructions.

3. **Upload `deposit_data-*.json`.** The launchpad checks the network, amounts, signatures, and that the keys weren't deposited before. If it rejects the file, don't work around it; the usual cause is keys made for another network.

4. **Connect your wallet** and check that it's on the right network (Ethereum mainnet or Hoodi).

5. **Send the deposit.** The launchpad creates one transaction per validator. In your wallet, check that each goes to the deposit contract above and that the amount matches (32 ETH for a standard validator).

6. **Keep the transaction hashes** the launchpad shows at the end.

</Steps>

## How long until it's active

After the deposit transaction is included, the beacon chain processes it, adds your validator, and activates it a few epochs later.

- With no queue, this takes about an hour or more.
- When many people stake at once, deposits wait in a queue, which can take days or weeks.

You can see the current entry queue on a block explorer such as [beaconcha.in](https://beaconcha.in) (or [hoodi.beaconcha.in](https://hoodi.beaconcha.in)). Keep your node running the whole time: once active, your validator has duties from its first epoch. For the full picture, see [How staking works](/learn/how-staking-works/).

## Check that it works

Ask your own beacon node about the validator:

```bash
curl -s http://127.0.0.1:3500/eth/v1/beacon/states/head/validators/<VALIDATOR_PUBKEY> | jq '.data.status'
```

| Result | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| `null` (the response says `Unknown validator`) | The deposit isn't processed yet. Wait. |
| `"pending_initialized"` or `"pending_queued"` | Deposit processed, waiting for activation. |
| `"active_ongoing"` | Active. Check that your validator attests. |

## Top up a validator

To add ETH to an existing validator (at least 1 ETH), open **Validator actions** on the launchpad (for example <a href={`${mainnet.launchpad}en/validator-actions`}>{`${mainnet.launchpad}en/validator-actions`}</a>), connect your wallet, pick the validator and choose **Add funds**.
{/* Verified against ethereum/staking-launchpad (dev branch, src/pages/Actions), 2026-09 */}

- A **regular (`0x01`)** validator counts at most 32 ETH; anything above is paid back out. Top up only if the balance fell below 32 ETH.
- A **compounding (`0x02`)** validator counts up to 2048 ETH, so a top-up raises its rewards.

## Troubleshooting

**The launchpad says the keys were already deposited.** Each key needs only one initial deposit. To add ETH, use the top-up flow.

**The deposit is included but the validator doesn't show up.** The queue may be long. Check the entry queue on a block explorer and wait.

**I deposited on the wrong network.** You can't move a deposit between networks. Create keys for the right network and deposit again.
